## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does a project usually take?**
Timing depends on the project scope, the integrations involved and how quickly business content and feedback are available. We agree on milestones before work begins.

**Do I need technical knowledge?**
No. We handle the setup, deployment and training. You only need to share your business information and the flow you want to improve.

**What data does the AI agent use?**
It uses the business information you provide: services, FAQs, process, pricing and common customer questions. It should never invent facts outside that context.

**What if it gives a wrong answer?**
The system is designed with guardrails. It should answer clearly within the business context and hand off to a human for more complex or sensitive matters.

**How does human handoff work?**
When the conversation needs a person, the AI can hand off with the customer context, contact details and requirement summary so your team can continue quickly.

**Where is data stored?**
Data handling depends on the services chosen for a project. We explain the providers, storage location and retention approach before launch; we do not ask customers to share sensitive information in chat.

**How is pricing structured?**
Pricing depends on scope, number of channels, ongoing support and automation needs. We keep it transparent and recommend the simplest setup that solves the bottleneck first.

**Can it speak Hindi or Gujarati?**
Yes. The assistant can reply in English, Hindi or Gujarati when the conversation is clearly in that language and the information is available.

**Can I cancel or pause the service?**
Yes. We keep the engagement simple and can pause, adjust or expand features as your needs change.

**Do you integrate with WhatsApp?**
Yes. We can connect website chat and WhatsApp flows so the same lead capture and handoff process works across channels.
